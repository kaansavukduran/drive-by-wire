## Font

Lexend, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The unchanged font and its licence are bundled as `assets/fonts/Lexend.ttf` and `assets/fonts/LEXEND-OFL.txt` (the licence text is also packed into the Windows, Linux and Android builds). Upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lexend . No network request is made for fonts.

## Artwork

Pixel art in `assets/` is generated by the project's own scripts in `tools/art/` and described in `assets/MANIFEST.json`. The owner's characters (Aion the axolotl, Accursed Wise Wolf, Blessed Ignorant Sheep, Baykuş Ömer) are used with the owner's permission; their identities and their source artwork remain the owner's. No third-party asset packs, downloaded sprites or downloaded audio are included.

## Build tools (not shipped inside the game)

- NSIS (zlib/libpng licence) builds the Windows installer; the installer contains the NSIS stub.
- appimagetool and the AppImage type-2 runtime (MIT) build the Linux AppImage; the AppImage contains that runtime.
