## Artwork

Artwork provenance is registered in `assets/PROVENANCE.json`. Procedural art uses the project's `tools/art/` scripts; the 0.7.0/0.10.x adaptations and0.11.0 unified game artwork use explicitly registered AI-assisted generation and are not regenerated by those scripts. Frame/contact registrations are in `data/story_cast_art.json` and `data/visual_art.json`. The owner's characters (Aion the axolotl, Accursed Wise Wolf, Blessed Ignorant Sheep, Baykuş Ömer) are used with the owner's permission; their identities and their source artwork remain the owner's. Tavşan Emre and Tavşan Eren remain separate owner characters; Eren’s orange fur, eyelashes, freckles, blazer and striped tie are preserved in the requested game adaptation. New artwork is OYUN/TASLAK and does not grant final canon approval. Private source reference images are not shipped. No third-party asset packs, downloaded sprites or downloaded audio are included.

## Build tools (not shipped inside the game)

- NSIS (zlib/libpng licence) builds the Windows installer; the installer contains the NSIS stub.
- appimagetool and the AppImage type-2 runtime (MIT) build the Linux AppImage; the AppImage contains that runtime.

## Control arrow glyphs (0.2.0)

`assets/fonts/ControlSymbols.ttf` is a renamed subset of DejaVu Sans containing U+2190–U+2193. It supplies missing arrow glyphs behind Lexend. Generated with fontTools; the Bitstream/DejaVu license and copyright are preserved in `assets/fonts/DEJAVU-LICENSE.txt`. Main character art is unchanged. Sound effects in `scripts/game/sounds.gd` are original procedural sine-wave cues, with no external samples.
